You can access [Genie One](/aws/en/genie-one/) from the *Databricks Connector for Google Sheets*. Ask questions about your governed Databricks data in natural language and bring the answers into Google Sheets as native rows and columns.

Genie answers questions about the governed data in your Databricks workspace, not the data in your spreadsheet.

important

Databricks AI agents might generate inaccurate responses. Verify before acting on agent output.

## Prerequisites

* Verify the *Databricks Connector for Google Sheets* is [set up](/aws/en/integrations/google-sheets/connect).
* You have access to Genie One.

## Chat with Genie

To open the Genie One interface, click the  Genie icon in the top-right corner of the *Databricks Connector for Google Sheets* sidebar.

1. Start chatting with Genie:
   * Start a new chat by typing a question.
   * Select a previous conversation from under **Recent** to continue it.
2. Ask your question in plain English.
3. Review the answer and check the citations.
4. Revisit anytime through conversation history, and delete chats you no longer need.

## Integration capabilities

### Import tabular results

Bring a table of Genie results directly into Google Sheets as native rows and columns.

1. When Genie returns a table, click **Import**.
2. Enter a name for the import.
3. Select the destination for the data.
4. Click **Save and Import**.

### View conversation history

Every conversation is saved, so you can revisit past chats and their answers.

1. In the Genie One interface in the connector sidebar, click the  conversation history icon.
2. Select the conversation you want to open.

### Delete a conversation

Permanently remove conversations you no longer need. Results already imported into your spreadsheet are not affected.

1. In the Genie One interface in the connector sidebar, click the  conversation history icon.
2. Click the delete button next to the conversation you want to delete.
