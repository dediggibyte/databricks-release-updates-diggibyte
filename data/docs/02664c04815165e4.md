Beta

This feature is in [Beta](/aws/en/release-notes/release-types). To use it, a workspace admin must turn on **Artifacts** from the **Previews** page. See [Manage Databricks previews](/aws/en/admin/workspace-settings/manage-previews).

Genie One builds slide decks from your data, with charts, text, and speaker notes, and saves them to your workspace as [artifacts](/aws/en/genie-one/artifacts). Ask Genie for a deck in chat, then edit the slides yourself in the slide editor, or ask Genie for more changes. When the deck is ready, present it with speaker notes, and use **Ask Genie** to answer your audience's questions about the data on any slide.

## Create a slide deck

To create a slide deck:

1. In a Genie One chat thread, ask Genie to create a slide deck. Describe the topic, the data to use, and the audience.

   prompt

   Tell Genie to do this for you:

   ```
   Using the samples.bakehouse sales data, create a slide deck for the leadership team that reviews sales by country and by product.
   ```
2. When Genie One finishes building the deck, it appears in the chat thread. Click the deck to open it.
3. To revise the deck, [edit the slides yourself](#edit), or ask Genie for changes in the same thread.

   prompt

   Tell Genie to do this for you:

   ```
   Add a slide that compares the top five franchises by revenue, and add speaker notes to each slide.
   ```

Genie One saves the deck to your workspace. In **My stuff** and **Discover**, it shows the **Slides** type.

## Edit slides

You can edit any part of a deck yourself in the slide editor, without asking Genie. Use it to adjust wording, layout, and charts before you present.

Slide decks open in **Edit** mode. If you're in another mode, switch the **Page mode** control to **Edit**. You need **Can edit** permission on the deck.

In **Edit** mode, use the left panel to see your slides and reorder them. To change text, move or resize elements, or insert charts, text boxes, and images, see [Edit an artifact](/aws/en/genie-one/artifacts#edit).

## Add speaker notes

In **Edit** mode, use the notes panel below the slide to add or change speaker notes for that slide. Speaker notes appear in **Presenter view** and not in the audience view.

## Present slides

To present a slide deck:

1. Open the deck.
2. Click **Present**. To choose how to present, click the arrow next to **Present** and select **Start from beginning** or **Presenter view**.
3. Use the presentation controls to move to the **Previous slide** or **Next slide**, or to switch to **Full screen**.
4. To stop presenting, click **End presentation**.

**Presenter view** opens a separate window with slide previews and your speaker notes, while the audience sees only the slides.

## Ask Genie while you present

When your audience asks a question about the data, use **Ask Genie** to answer it without leaving the presentation. Genie answers from the data behind the slide, so you can respond to follow-up questions on the spot.

To ask Genie while you present:

1. Click **Ask Genie**.
2. Select an element on the slide, such as a chart or a number.
3. Type your question and click **Ask**. For example, select a sales-by-country chart and ask "Which product sold the most in Japan?"
4. When Genie answers, a notification appears. Click **View** to see the answer.

The presentation keeps running while Genie works on the answer. For who can use **Ask Genie** and where answers appear, see [Ask Genie about an artifact](/aws/en/genie-one/artifacts#ask-genie).

## Share a deck

To share a slide deck, open it and click **Share**, then add users or groups with **Can read** or **Can edit** permission, or choose **Anyone in the workspace can view**. Anyone who can view the deck can see its snapshot data. See [Share an artifact](/aws/en/genie-one/artifacts#share) and [Data in artifacts](/aws/en/genie-one/artifacts#data).
