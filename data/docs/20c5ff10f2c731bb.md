A *Page* is a governed, authoritative definition of a business concept, such as a critical term, entity, or acronym. Pages are built into the [Discover page](/aws/en/discover/discover-page) and organized by [domain and subdomain](/aws/en/uc-semantics/domains). Each domain and subdomain has its own set of Pages, and users with access to a domain can create and govern them.

Pages are part of [Unity Catalog semantics](/aws/en/uc-semantics/): the business context that you define and govern explicitly, forming the modeled knowledge in [Genie Ontology](/aws/en/genie/genie-ontology/). When Genie One answers a question about a concept you've defined in a Page, it prioritizes the Page's definition over learned knowledge, and cites the Page so users can confirm the source. See [How Genie One uses Pages](#genie).

To document a single table instead of a concept, use a [wiki](/aws/en/uc-semantics/wikis) (Beta).

## What's in a Page

A Page contains the following fields:

| Field | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Domain | The domain or subdomain the Page belongs to. |
| Owner | The user responsible for the Page. The creator is the owner by default. |
| Synonyms | Alternative names for the concept. |
| Description | A short summary of the concept. |
| Page body | Freeform, rich-text content that supports links, images, tables, and inline `@` tagging of Unity Catalog and workspace assets. |
| Related assets | A list of Databricks assets (workspace and Unity Catalog objects) that logically connect to, depend on, or contextualize the Page, such as parent and child Pages, dependent metrics, or associated tables. |
| Sources | A list of Databricks and external assets (workspace and Unity Catalog objects, links) that serve as the origin of the Page content. |

## Permissions

A Page inherits access from the domain or subdomain it belongs to: anyone who can access the domain can read its published Pages. You don't grant permissions on a Page directly. What a user can do with a domain's Pages depends on their persona:

| Persona | Access |
| --- | --- |
| [Consumer](/aws/en/data-governance/unity-catalog/data-discovery#personas) | Consumers can view, comment on, and suggest edits to published Pages that they don't own. They can also create draft Pages, which only a Page manager or curator can publish. Consumer is the baseline persona for users without any other permissions on the **Discover** page. |
| Page owner | A user who creates a draft becomes its Page owner. A Page owner can edit their own draft Pages. After a Page manager or curator publishes a consumer's draft, the consumer can no longer edit it, but stays in the **Owner** field as the point of contact. |
| Page manager | Page managers can edit, publish, unpublish, and delete Pages, accept or reject suggestions, delete comments, and change the Page owner, for any Page in the scope where they hold `MANAGE PAGES`. In the same scope, they can view all draft Pages. See [Grant Page manager permissions](#grant-page-manager-permissions). |
| [Curator](/aws/en/data-governance/unity-catalog/data-discovery#personas) | Curators can do everything a Page manager can do, on any Page in the domains and subdomains where they hold curator permissions (`MANAGE DISCOVER` at the account level, or `MANAGE DOMAIN` on a domain or subdomain). Curators can also certify and deprecate Pages. See [Curator permissions](/aws/en/uc-semantics/domains#manage-discovery). |

Account admins and workspace admins automatically hold account-level `MANAGE DISCOVER` and act as curators over every domain and Page. Account admins can grant `MANAGE DISCOVER` to others at the account level, or `MANAGE DOMAIN` on a specific domain or subdomain. See [Curator permissions](/aws/en/uc-semantics/domains#manage-discovery).

### Grant Page manager permissions

By default, `MANAGE PAGES` is granted to **All account users** on every domain, including newly created domains. To limit who can publish and manage Pages in a specific domain and its subdomains, revoke `MANAGE PAGES` from **All account users** on that domain and grant it to specific users, groups, or service principals.

Only curators can grant and revoke `MANAGE PAGES`. A curator with `MANAGE DISCOVER` can grant or revoke it on any domain or subdomain. A curator with `MANAGE DOMAIN` can grant or revoke it only on the domains and subdomains where they hold `MANAGE DOMAIN`. You can grant `MANAGE PAGES` at three scopes, from broadest to narrowest:

| Scope | A Page manager with this grant can manage |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Every Page in every domain and subdomain in the account. |
| Domain | Every Page in the domain and its subdomains. |
| Subdomain | Every Page in the subdomain. |

To grant or revoke `MANAGE PAGES` at the account level:

1. On the **Discover** page, click the  overflow menu and select **Manage Domains**.
2. Click the **Permissions** tab, then click **Grant Permissions**.
3. Select the principal, select **Manage Pages** under **Permissions**, and click **Save**.
4. To revoke the permission, click the **x** on the permission next to the principal.

To grant or revoke `MANAGE PAGES` on a domain or subdomain:

1. On the **Discover** page, open the domain. To scope the grant to a subdomain, open the subdomain from the domain page.
2. Click the  overflow menu and select **Domain permissions**.
3. Select the principal, select **Manage Pages** under **Permissions**, and click **Save**.
4. To revoke the permission, click the **x** on the permission next to the principal.

## Create a Page

Every Page is attached to a domain or subdomain, so you need at least one domain set up before you can create Pages. See [Create a domain](/aws/en/uc-semantics/domains#domain-creation).

You can author a Page manually or generate one with Genie Code from source material.

warning

Page data is encrypted at rest with Databricks-managed keys. Pages don't support customer-managed key (CMK) encryption. Because Pages are an account-level feature, Page data may be replicated globally to support workspaces in different regions. Do not include PII, regulated data, or other sensitive information in Page names, descriptions, synonyms, or body text.

1. To access Pages, click **Discover**, then click **Pages**.
2. Click **Create page** to open the Page editor.
3. Confirm or select the **Domain**. The domain is filled in automatically if you opened the editor from within a domain, and left empty otherwise.
4. Add the Page content:

   * To author manually, fill in the structured fields and Page body.
   * To generate the Page with Genie Code, use the Genie Code pane in the Page editor. You can also open the pane from the **Discover** page or a specific domain page. Attach source material in the pane, which can include external content accessible through MCP connections, public links, files, and Unity Catalog and workspace assets. After Genie Code drafts the structured fields and Page body, attach the appropriate source material under **Sources** and **Related assets**.
5. Save your work as a draft anytime by clicking **Save as draft**. If you're a Page manager or curator, click **Publish** to publish the Page. Otherwise, a Page manager or curator publishes your draft. See [Edit and publish Pages in draft](#publish-page).

## Bulk import Pages

If your organization already documents its terms somewhere else, you can create Pages from those documents instead of writing each one by hand. Genie Code reads the documents you attach, extracts the terms it finds, and returns a set of proposed Pages. You review and edit the proposed Pages before any of them are created.

1. To access Pages, click **Discover**, then click **Pages**.
2. Click **Create page** to open the Page editor.
3. If the Genie Code pane isn't already open, click  in the upper-right corner of the page to open it.
4. In the Genie Code pane, click **Bulk import pages**.
5. Follow the prompts. Tell Genie Code which domains you want to add Pages to, and what knowledge sources to use. To attach source material, click  in the chat box, then use any of the following:

   * **Attach files**: Files from your local machine.
   * **Add context**: Unity Catalog and workspace objects.
   * **Connections**: External content accessible through MCP connections. You also manage your connections from here.

   You can also paste public links directly into the chat box.
6. Genie Code processes the sources and returns a deduplicated set of proposed Pages for review. You can resolve flags such as conflicts, duplicates, and low-confidence terms, edit the content, and exclude Pages you don't want.
7. When you finish reviewing, Genie Code creates all of the Pages at once as drafts. You can then review, edit, and publish them under their domains. See [Edit and publish Pages in draft](#publish-page).

## Edit and publish Pages in draft

The Page owner, a Page manager, or a curator can edit a draft. Only a Page manager or curator can publish it. See [Permissions](#permissions).

1. Click **Discover**, then click **Pages**.
2. Only published Pages are shown by default. Click **Show drafts** to view all Pages currently in draft.
3. Click the Page you want to edit or publish.
4. Make edits to the Page by clicking **Edit**, or publish the Page by clicking **Publish**.

Page managers and curators can unpublish and republish a Page at any time. To unpublish a Page, click the  overflow menu on a published Page and select **Unpublish**.

## Collaborate on a Page

[Consumers](/aws/en/data-governance/unity-catalog/data-discovery#personas) can collaborate on published Pages:

* **Comment**: Ask a follow-up question or flag context for the owner. You can edit or delete your own comments. Page managers and curators can delete anyone's comments on a published Page.
* **Suggest edits**: Suggest changes to a published Page's body. Each time you click **Suggestion**, edit the body, and click **Save**, your edits are grouped into a single batch. There are no inline suggestions. A Page manager or curator accepts or rejects the entire batch at once. Accepting a batch clears every other pending batch on the Page, including batches from other users, and this can't be undone.
* **React**: Upvote or downvote a Page to signal whether it answered your question.

Page managers and curators can also edit a published Page's content directly, bypassing the suggestion workflow.

## Certify or deprecate a Page

Certify a Page to mark it as an authoritative, trusted definition, or deprecate it to warn users that its content is outdated. Pages use the same `system.certification_status` system tag as other data assets. See [Flag data as certified or deprecated](/aws/en/data-governance/unity-catalog/certify-deprecate-data).

To certify or deprecate a Page, you must have curator permissions on its domain or subdomain. See [Curator permissions](/aws/en/uc-semantics/domains#manage-discovery).

1. Open the Page, click the  overflow menu, and select **Assign certification**.
2. Select **Certified** or **Deprecated**.
3. Click **Save**.

The certified or deprecated icon appears next to the Page name. To change or remove the status, repeat these steps.
