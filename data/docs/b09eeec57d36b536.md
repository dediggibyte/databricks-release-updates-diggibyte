This article describes Databricks Designated Services, which are features in Databricks that use [Databricks Geos](/aws/en/resources/databricks-geos) to manage data residency when processing customer content.

important

If a Designated Service is available in your workspace's Geo, then your content is processed in that Geo. Some Designated Services are not natively available in all Geos, and admins might need to enable [cross-Geo processing](/aws/en/resources/databricks-geos#cross-geo-processing) before these Designated Services can be used. If you enable cross-Geo processing on your workspace, the Designated Service can process your content in other Geos. Importantly, your data is not stored outside of your workspace's Geo regardless of this setting.

For [AI assistive features](/aws/en/databricks-ai/) in US and EU regions (regardless of cross-Geo processing setting), workspaces in the Americas use a US-based model, and workspaces in Europe use a Europe-based model.
