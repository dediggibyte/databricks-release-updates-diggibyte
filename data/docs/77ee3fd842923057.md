The Genie One desktop app is a native app for macOS and Windows that allows you to access Genie One on your desktop. The desktop app has the same capabilities as the web experience plus desktop conveniences like window navigation controls, automatic updates, and, on macOS, a dock badge for unread activity.

The desktop app works alongside the Genie One web experience. You can ask data questions in natural language, work with your threads and documents, use Genie Agents and Unity Catalog tables, and run Databricks Apps.

## Install the app

Download the installer for your operating system, then follow the steps for your platform.

### macOS

[Download the Genie One desktop app for macOS](https://genie-desktop.cloud.databricks.com/download/darwin/arm64/GenieOne-latest.dmg)

After the download completes, open the installer (`.dmg`) and follow the prompts to install the app.

### Windows

[Download the Genie One desktop app for Windows](https://genie-desktop.cloud.databricks.com/download/win32/x64/GenieOne-latest-Setup.exe)

After the download completes, run the installer (`GenieOne-latest-Setup.exe`). The installer sets up the app and opens it when it finishes.

## Sign in

When you open the app, sign in one of two ways:

* Select **Log in** to sign in with your Databricks credentials. The app shows an account selector, then a workspace selector, so you can choose which workspace to sign in to.
* Select **Enter workspace link**, enter your workspace URL, and select **Enter** to sign in directly to that workspace.

Use **Enter workspace link** to sign in directly to a workspace when the standard **Log in** flow doesn't work for your account. This can happen when missing account single sign-on (SSO) or email verification prevent sign-in.

The app remembers your workspace, so later launches take you straight back to Genie One. Signing out clears your session in the app.

## Differences between the desktop app and the web experience

The desktop app runs the same Genie One as the browser, with a few desktop-specific behaviors:

* A title bar at the top of the window provides **Back**, **Forward**, and **Reload** controls for moving through Genie One.
* On macOS, the app shows a badge on its dock icon with the number of threads that have unread messages. To clear the badge, click the dock icon with the right mouse button and select **Clear notifications**, or sign out.
* Links to Genie One pages open in the app. Links to other parts of the Databricks workspace, such as settings, SQL, jobs, or compute, and links to sites outside Databricks, open in your default browser.
* The app keeps itself up to date, downloading new versions in the background.
* The app's interface follows your operating system's language setting.

## Limitations

* The desktop app is available on macOS and Windows.
* The unread-activity badge and the **Clear notifications** option are available on macOS only.
* Databricks does not provide an admin control to enable, disable, or restrict the desktop app for your organization. The app is another way to access Genie One, and access is managed the same way as other clients, such as through your organization's mobile device management (MDM).
* Signing out clears your session in the app, but your workspace session remains valid until it expires.
